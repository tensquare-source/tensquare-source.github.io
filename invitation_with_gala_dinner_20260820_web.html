The Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Authority (MPFA), together with the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) and the International Organisation of Pension Supervisors (IOPS), will co-host the 2026 OECD/IOPS/MPFA Global Forum on Private Pensions to be held in Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (SAR). This prestigious two-day Global Forum, scheduled for 8 and 9 December 2026, will discuss how to empower global pension systems through good design and innovation, and bring together senior officials from supervisory authorities in OECD and IOPS member jurisdictions, alongside distinguished experts from international organisations, pension fund industry and leading research institutes.

MPFA is an active member of IOPS, the international standard-setting body for pension supervision with participation from over 80 jurisdictions worldwide, and is honoured to co-host this important event.

We are exceptionally privileged that the Honourable John Lee, Chief Executive of the Hong Kong SAR, will officiate at the Opening Ceremony and deliver an opening address for this occasion.

You are cordially invited to attend the Global Forum, details of which are as follows:

Date: 8-9 December 2026 (Tue-Wed)

The Honourable Paul Chan, Financial Secretary of the Hong Kong SAR will be the Guest of Honour and deliver a keynote speech.
